Title: Halloween at the Court Below
 
Saturday, Oct 31
 
8:00pm - 2:00am
 
Hades and Persephone request your presence:
 
For one night, we are opening the gates of the Underworld for a night of dark elegance, decadent treats, and lingering spirits.
 
Olympus has received their summons—unwilling as they may be to make the descent. The dead require no invitation, yet hold one regardless. As for mortals? You are welcome, provided you can find the door.
 
Join us beneath the earth for an evening of libations, sweets, and nocturnal revelry.
 
The Dress Code:
 
Myths, Legends, & Shades Interpret the theme as grandly or as subtly as you wish-effort is honored, but your presence is what matters most.
 
The High Pantheon: Gods, goddesses, heroes, and mythological figures from any realm.
 
- The Ancients: Generic or specific Greek and Roman attire (from toga-clad philosophers to legionnaires).
 
The Departed: Ghosts, shades, or any creature inhabiting the underworlds of myth.
 
If all else fails, dress in your finest dark, elegant attire and claim you are an unannounced ghost.
 
Food & Beverage
 
Wine, sinister cocktails, and an array of savoury snacks and sweet treats to keep you from fading into the ether.
 
No full dinner will be served, so dine before you descend
